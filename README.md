# Maliyet Hesap — Quick Cost Check

Quick monthly cost totals in Turkish lira, from maliyethesap.com.

A quick companion to maliyethesap.com (Maliyet Hesap). Add up monthly costs in Turkish lira and see the annual total at a glance. Everything runs locally; nothing is sent or stored.

## Download

Installable packages are on the [Releases](https://github.com/maria876david-debug/maliyet-hesap-extension/releases) page — Microsoft Edge (.zip) and Firefox (.zip).

## Website

https://maliyethesap.com

## Privacy

No data is collected. The extension runs locally in your browser; no account, no tracking, no remote requests.
